# Fred Hutchinson Cancer Center - Employment & Cover Letters

This repository compiles professional application materials, research proposals, personal statements, and targeted cover letters developed by **Dr. Correo Hofstad** for diverse roles at the Fred Hutchinson Cancer Center (FHCC).

The documents cover:
*   **Executive Vision & Research Leadership:** Including leukemia leadership, breast oncology, and Cancer AI Alliance roles.
*   **Scientific & Technical Roles:** Focusing on UV-C vesicle sanitization research, systems engineering, clinical data, and finance.
*   **Clinical & Ops Positions:** Covering BMT, APP, HR Business Partner, and Senior IT PMO roles.

## ⚙️ Repository Structure
Documents are provided in both editable `.docx` formats and standardized `.pdf` for reference.
